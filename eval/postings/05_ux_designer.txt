Product Designer
Austin, TX (hybrid)

About the role
We are a team of four designers supporting eight engineering squads. You will
own the design system and the clinician-facing scheduling experience.

Responsibilities
- Maintain and extend our Figma component library, used by every squad
- Design the scheduling workflow used by clinic staff, who are often working
  under time pressure with interruptions
- Run usability sessions with clinical staff and synthesize findings
- Partner with engineering on accessibility, including WCAG 2.1 AA compliance

Qualifications
- 4+ years designing complex workflow software, not marketing sites
- Portfolio showing how you handled a genuinely constrained problem
- Experience with design systems at scale
- Familiarity with healthcare settings is preferred but not required
