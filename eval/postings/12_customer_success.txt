Customer Success Manager, Enterprise
Remote (US)

About the role
You will own a book of roughly 25 enterprise accounts representing $8M in
annual recurring revenue.

Responsibilities
- Drive adoption and renewal across your accounts, and own the renewal forecast
- Run quarterly business reviews with executive stakeholders
- Identify expansion opportunities and partner with sales to close them
- Act as the voice of the customer to product, with evidence rather than anecdote

Qualifications
- 4+ years in customer success or account management at an enterprise software company
- Experience managing renewals and forecasting accurately
- Comfort with data; you should be able to pull usage reports yourself
- Strong written communication
