Site Reliability Engineer
Remote (EU)

About the role
Our platform runs on Kubernetes across three regions. You will be responsible
for the reliability of the services that sit under every product team.

What you will do
- Own our Kubernetes platform, including cluster upgrades and capacity planning
- Reduce mean time to recovery, which is currently over 40 minutes
- Build and maintain Terraform modules that product teams consume
- Improve our observability stack (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry)
- Lead incident response and write blameless postmortems

Requirements
- 5+ years in SRE, platform or infrastructure engineering
- Production experience with Kubernetes beyond running kubectl
- Strong skills in at least one of Go or Python
- Experience with infrastructure as code in a team setting
