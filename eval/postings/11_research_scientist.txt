Research Scientist, Speech
Seattle, WA

About the role
Our speech team works on recognition quality for low-resource languages.

Responsibilities
- Design and run experiments on acoustic and language modeling for languages
  with under 100 hours of labelled audio
- Publish findings at venues such as Interspeech and ICASSP
- Transfer successful research into the production recognition stack
- Mentor interns and collaborate with engineering on evaluation infrastructure

Qualifications
- PhD in speech, machine learning or a related field, or equivalent experience
- Publication record in speech or language processing
- Strong experimental discipline, including careful ablation studies
- Experience with self-supervised pretraining approaches
