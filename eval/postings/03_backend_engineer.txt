Senior Backend Engineer, Payments
New York, NY

About the role
You will work on the services that move money for our merchants. Correctness
and auditability matter more than velocity here.

Responsibilities
- Design and build idempotent APIs in Go that handle payment authorization,
  capture and refund flows
- Own the reconciliation process against our payment processors, including the
  daily settlement job
- Write runbooks and participate in a weekly on-call rotation
- Lead design reviews and mentor two junior engineers

Qualifications
- 6+ years of backend experience, with at least 2 in payments, banking or
  another regulated domain
- Deep understanding of database transactions, isolation levels and locking
- Experience with event-driven architectures using Kafka
- Track record of operating services with strict uptime commitments
