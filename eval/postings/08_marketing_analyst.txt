Marketing Analyst
Denver, CO

About the role
You will measure whether our marketing spend works, and tell us honestly when
it does not.

What you will do
- Build attribution reporting across paid search, paid social and email
- Run and analyze incrementality tests, including geo holdouts
- Maintain the weekly marketing performance review deck
- Partner with finance on budget forecasting

Qualifications
- 2+ years in marketing analytics or a similar quantitative role
- Proficiency with SQL and spreadsheet modeling
- Understanding of the limits of last-click attribution
- Ability to communicate findings clearly to a non-technical audience
