Product Manager, Onboarding
San Francisco, CA

The opportunity
Activation is our biggest leak. Fewer than a third of signups reach the point
where they see value. You will own that problem end to end.

What you will do
- Define and track the activation metric, and be accountable for moving it
- Run discovery interviews with new users and translate what you hear into a
  prioritized roadmap
- Work with design and engineering to ship experiments weekly
- Communicate results and tradeoffs to the executive team

What we are looking for
- 3+ years of product management experience in a B2B SaaS environment
- Comfort with SQL and experimentation frameworks; you should be able to pull
  your own numbers
- Experience running a structured discovery process
- Strong written communication; we write memos, not slide decks
